# Send financial emails deliberately

Issuing an invoice records the issued invoice. It does not automatically email the parent or create a parent notification. Recording a payment, verifying or rejecting a voucher, and preparing a draft also do not automatically send financial emails or notifications. Scheduled invoice reminder emails are disabled; booking/session reminders remain separate.

## Send an invoice

Open the invoice, review its details and issue the draft when appropriate. Choose **Send invoice by email** separately when you intend to email the parent. Issuing and emailing are distinct actions; an issued invoice does not prove an email was sent.

## Send a receipt

Find the verified payment in the invoice's payment history and choose **Send receipt by email**. Review the displayed recipient address, then choose **Confirm send receipt** to send, or **Cancel** to leave it unsent. Add or correct the parent's email address before sending if it is missing or wrong. Opening the confirmation does not send the email.

Sending a receipt is a separate action from recording the payment. Financial records remain unchanged by the send action.

## Interpret sending status

**Accepted by the sending service** means the sending service accepted the request; it does not confirm inbox delivery. If the outcome is unconfirmed, check the recorded sending status before trying again because the service may already have accepted the email.

Previous sending history remains visible. These changes cannot recall an email previously accepted by the sending service, and an older accepted entry does not mean a new email was sent.
