# Family billing cycles and payment corrections

## Set up a family's billing cycle

1. Open **Parents**, search for the parent, and open their profile.
2. Choose **Set up billing cycle** (or **Manage billing cycle** for an existing plan). This opens the profile's **Finance / Ledger** section.
3. If the family uses multiple centres, choose **Billing centre**. Each centre has its own fee and billing cycle.
4. Enter the monthly family fee and first billing period start date. Review invoice preparation timing and the children covered, then choose **Save billing cycle**.

Saving makes the plan eligible for scheduled draft preparation. It does not issue or email an invoice. For an active plan with a positive fee and covered children, choose **Review first draft invoice** or **Review / prepare draft invoice** and review the period, amount and dates before the separate draft action. Follow the existing invoice review and issue workflow when ready to send an invoice.

If no accessible centre is available, check the children's centre and enrolment through **Overview**. Imported details marked for review must be verified before setup.

## Correct a recorded payment date or reference

1. Open **Finance**, open the invoice and find **Payment history**.
2. On an eligible payment, choose **Correct payment details**.
3. Correct **Payment date**, **Payment reference**, or both. Dates use the Europe/London calendar and cannot be in the future.
4. Enter a meaningful **Reason for correction**, then choose **Save correction**.
5. Check the updated payment and **Correction history**. Open **Receipt** again to download the corrected receipt; previously downloaded copies do not update themselves.

Corrections are available only to an Owner or an authorised Manager with access to the invoice's centre, for verified manually recorded payments on a non-void invoice. Processor payments are excluded. The amount, payment status and paid balance stay unchanged. The original and corrected values, reason and actor are audited. No email is sent automatically.

If the payment changed while you were editing, or saving was not confirmed, reload the invoice and review the current payment before retrying.

## Choose the appropriate action

- **Correct payment details** fixes a date or reference while preserving the payment amount and paid balance.
- **Reverse payment entry** removes the entry from paid totals and can reopen the invoice. It does not refund money.
- **Void invoice** is an invoice cancellation action; it is not a payment date correction.
