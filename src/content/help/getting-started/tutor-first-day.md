# SprintScale CMS — Quick Start Guide
## Tutor / Club Leader: First Day Checklist

**Target Audience:** New Tutors and Classroom Activity Leaders  
**Estimated Time:** 15 Minutes  
**Goal:** Learn how to check in children, view medical badges, take attendance roll calls, and know the safeguarding escalation route on your first day.

---

## First Day Operational Checklist

```
  [08:00 / Arrival]    Step 1: Sign in at `/login` and select your centre
  [Before Session]     Step 2: Check today's schedule and note allergy flags
  [Session Start]      Step 3: Open Attendance Roll Call / Kiosk Mode
  [During Session]     Step 4: Mark arrivals, latecomers, and absences
  [Pickup / Depart]    Step 5: Tap "Check Out" as parents collect children
  [Ongoing]            Step 6: Know your Safeguarding Lead escalation route
```

---

### Step 1: Sign In to SprintScale CMS

![Figure — Staff Invitation Acceptance Page with password and profile setup](/training/assets/screenshots/annotated/SS-D6-S052.png)
*Figure QS-T.1 — Staff Invitation Acceptance Screen*

📹 **Video Walkthrough:** [Watch: Accepting a Staff Email Invitation](/training/assets/videos/SS-D6-V023.mp4)
1. On your tablet or phone browser, go to: `https://app.sprintscaleit.co.uk/login`.
2. Enter your staff email address and password (or use Google Sign-In).
3. If this is your first time, check your email for the invitation link sent by your Owner/Manager.

---

### Step 2: Review Today's Session & Allergy Badges
1. Open the **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`).
2. Look at **Today's Schedule** to see how many children are expected.
3. Pay close attention to any highlighted **Medical Badges**:
   - 🔴 **Red Badges:** Severe allergies (e.g. EpiPen, Peanut Allergy) or medical conditions (e.g. Asthma, Diabetes).
   - 🟡 **Yellow Badges:** Dietary requirements (e.g. Halal, Gluten-free, Dairy-free).

---

### Step 3: Open the Live Attendance Register

![Figure — Daily Attendance Register with attendee roster, status badges, and timelogs](/training/assets/screenshots/annotated/SS-D6-S014.png)
*Figure QS-T.2 — Daily Attendance Register*

📹 **Video Walkthrough:** [Watch: Marking Morning and Afternoon Class Register](/training/assets/videos/SS-D6-V006.mp4)
1. Tap `Sidebar → Attendance` (`/dashboard/attendance`).
2. As each child walks into the club room:
   - Tap **Check In** next to their name.
   - The card updates with a green **Present** badge and records the exact time.
3. If a tablet is mounted at the door, tap `Sidebar → Kiosk` for a fast, full-screen touch check-in screen.

---

### Step 4: Handling Late Arrivals & Absences
- **If a child arrives late:** Tap **Check In**. The system automatically notes the late arrival (e.g. *15m late*).
- **If an expected child is not attending:** Tap **Absent** and select the reason given by the school or parent (`Illness`, `Holiday`, `Family`, or `Other`).

---

### Step 5: Recording Departures at Pickup Time
> [!SAFEGUARDING]
> Never release a child to an unverified adult. If an unfamiliar adult arrives, ask your Centre Manager or Front Desk to verify their Authorised Collector name and password.

1. When the collecting adult arrives, locate the child on `/dashboard/attendance` (or `/dashboard/kiosk`).
2. Tap **Check Out**.
3. Confirm that 100% of children show **Checked Out** before leaving the premises.

---

### Step 6: Safeguarding Escalation on Day One
- **If a child is injured:** Immediately apply first aid and inform Front Desk or the Centre Manager to log an official accident report.
- **If a child makes a disclosure:** Listen calmly without asking leading questions. **Immediately report the disclosure in person to your Centre Manager (Designated Safeguarding Lead).** Do not type safeguarding notes in general classroom notes.

---

## Expected Outcome on Day One

✅ You can log into your tablet and view today's roll call.  
✅ You know how to check in arrivals and record pickups.  
✅ You are aware of all medical and allergy alerts in your classroom.  
✅ You know exactly who your Centre Manager / Designated Safeguarding Lead is.
