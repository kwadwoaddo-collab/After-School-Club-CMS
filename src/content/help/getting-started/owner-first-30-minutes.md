# SprintScale CMS — Quick Start Guide
## Organisation Owner: The First 30 Minutes

**Target Audience:** New Organisation Owners  
**Estimated Time:** 30 Minutes  
**Goal:** Configure your organisation, establish your first club centre, invite your management team, and prepare your public booking links.

---

## 30-Minute Readiness Checklist

```
  [00:00 - 05:00]  Step 1: Sign in and complete organisation profile
  [05:00 - 10:00]  Step 2: Configure your first club centre and banking details
  [10:00 - 15:00]  Step 3: Define centre operating hours and session capacities
  [15:00 - 20:00]  Step 4: Invite your Centre Manager and assign centre access
  [20:00 - 25:00]  Step 5: Copy and test your public booking & registration links
  [25:00 - 30:00]  Step 6: Review Dashboard KPIs and bookmark your cockpit
```

---

### Step 1: Complete Organisation Profile (Minutes 0–5)
1. Sign in at `https://app.sprintscaleit.co.uk/login` using your Owner credentials or Google account.
2. If this is your first sign-in, you will be automatically directed to `/onboarding`.
3. Enter your legal **Organisation Name** (e.g. *Sydenham After School Club LTD*).
4. Select your **Primary Brand Color** (used across parent portal headers and invoices).
5. Upload your high-resolution **Club Logo** (PNG or JPG).
6. Click **Save & Continue**.

---

### Step 2: Configure Centre Details & Bank Account (Minutes 5–10)
1. Navigate to: `Sidebar → Centres → [Select Your Centre]`.
2. Click **Centre Settings** (`/dashboard/centres/[id]/settings`):
   - Enter physical venue address, contact phone, and **Ofsted Registration Number**.
3. Click the **Billing Tab** (`/dashboard/centres/[id]/billing`):
   - Enter your **Bank Name**, **Sort Code**, and **Account Number** (these print on parent invoices).
   - Enter the **Billing Email** where remittance notifications should be sent.
4. Click **Save Centre Configuration**.

---

### Step 3: Set Operating Hours & Slots (Minutes 10–15)
1. Navigate to: `Sidebar → Availability`.
2. Click **Edit Hours** next to your centre.
3. Toggle the days your club operates (e.g. Monday to Friday).
4. Set daily session start and finish times (e.g. `15:30` to `18:00`).
5. Click **Save Operating Schedule**.

---

### Step 4: Invite Your Centre Manager (Minutes 15–20)
1. Navigate to: `Sidebar → Team → [+ Invite Staff]`.
2. Enter your manager's email address.
3. Select Role: **Manager**.
4. Check the box for your newly configured Centre.
5. Click **Send Invitation**.
6. Your manager will receive an immediate email with an activation link.

---

### Step 5: Test Public Booking & Registration Links (Minutes 20–25)
1. Navigate to: `Sidebar → Share Portals` (`/dashboard/share`).
2. Click **Copy Link** under **Public Booking Portal** (e.g. `https://app.sprintscaleit.co.uk/book/your-org`).
3. Click **Copy Link** under **Public Registration Form** (e.g. `https://app.sprintscaleit.co.uk/register/your-org`).
4. Paste the links into your school newsletter, website, or social media channels for parent intake.

---

## Expected Outcome After 30 Minutes

✅ Your organisation is live and branded with your logo and colors.  
✅ Your first club centre is established with Ofsted ID, bank account details, and opening hours.  
✅ Your Centre Manager is invited and provisioned with centre-specific access.  
✅ Your public registration and booking URLs are ready to accept parent enrolments.
